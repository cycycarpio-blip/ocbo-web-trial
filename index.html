Office of the City Building Official

City Government of Puerto Princesa

OUR BEGINNINGS

Created on January 1, 2018, by virtue of City Ordinance No. 856, the OCBO emerged as a distinct office from the Office of the City Engineer, merging the Zoning Division and the Building and Public Safety Section. Under the leadership of Engr. Rex G. Bundac, the office ensures strict enforcement of the National Building Code and Zoning Ordinances.

MANDATE

  • Administration and enforcement of the National Building Code (P.D. 1096), its IRR, and the Philippine Green Building Code.
  • Strict implementation of the City Zoning Ordinance (Ordinance No. 560) and HLURB regulations.
  • Enforcement of the Accessibility Law (BP 344) for an inclusive built environment.

SERVICE PLEDGE

We commit to:

  • Serve with loyalty, integrity, and the highest degree of professionalism.
  • Provide efficient, customer-oriented public service.
  • Maintain transparent, fair, and God-centered standards.
  • Simplify procedures to reduce red tape and expedite transactions.
  • Serve all applicants present within premises, even during lunch breaks.

VISION

An organization that promotes and protects the health, safety, peace, and general welfare of Puerto Princesa through strict building standards.

MISSION

To safeguard life and property through sound environmental management and technical competence, guiding the city's growth in accordance with the Comprehensive Land Use Plan.

Permit Requirements

Ensure you have all documents ready before visiting the office or applying online

New Building Permit

(Single Dwelling Residential/Commercial/Industrial/Others)

  • Duly accomplished and Notarized Unified Application and Ancillary Permits (Civil/Structural, Plumbing, Electrical, Architectural Permits, etc.) (4 original copies, back to back)
  • Updated Certified True Copy of OCT/TCT by the City Registry of Deeds...
    Note: Submit Certification from the Socialized Housing (HOAI/Subdivision) if applicable
    • In the absence of any existing Certificate of Title...
    • If City Housing Project: A Certificate from the City Housing...
  • Building Plans Design, Bill of Materials and Estimates...
    • Architectural Plans (Architect)
    • Civil/Structural Plans (Civil Engineer)
      • Structural Design and Analysis (above 20m2 floor area)
      • Boring Test (3 floors & up)
    • Sanitary/Plumbing Plans (Master Plumber/Sanitary Engineer)
    • Electrical Plans (Professional Electrical Engineer)
    • If applicable only:
    • Mechanical Plans (Professional Mechanical Engineer)
    • Electronics Plans (Professional Electronics Communications Engineer)
    • Geodetic Documents/Line & Grade
    • Fire Protection Plan
  • Valid Licenses (PRC ID) & current PTR...
  • PALECO Clearance in compliance to RA 11361...
  • Long Folder (1 pc) and Expanding envelope (2 pcs)
The following documents are to be submitted, if necessary:
  • Proposed Projects (not yet started):
    DOLE's Construction Safety and Health Program (CSHP), NBC MC# 2, Series of 2011 (1 original 3 photocopies)
  • Authorized Representatives:
    A Sworn Special Power of Attorney for the representative to file/follow up/claim decision on the application with valid ID (1 original 4 photocopies)
  • Barangay Clearance interposing no objection to the proposed/existing project (1 original 3 photocopies)
  • Height Clearance from CAAP / Road-Right-of-Way Clearance from DPWH (if fronting National Road) (1 original 3 photocopies)
  • Affidavit of Undertaking for submission of required clearances/permits from other agencies per NBCP (1 original photocopy)
